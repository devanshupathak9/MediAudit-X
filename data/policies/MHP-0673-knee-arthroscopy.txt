MERIDIAN HEALTH PLAN
CLINICAL POLICY BULLETIN MHP-0673
ARTHROSCOPIC SURGERY OF THE KNEE
Version 2026.1 | Effective 2026-01-01 | Supersedes 2025.2

NOTE: This is a SYNTHETIC policy document written for the MediAudit-X demo. It is
modeled on the structure of publicly published payer medical policies but describes
a fictional payer and is not the coverage policy of any real organization.

SECTION 1. SCOPE

This policy applies to arthroscopic procedures of the knee, including but not
limited to CPT codes 29870, 29873, 29874, 29877, 29880, and 29881, when performed
for degenerative joint disease or meniscal pathology. It does not apply to
arthroscopy performed for acute traumatic injury sustained within the preceding
fourteen (14) days, which is reviewed under policy MHP-0681.

SECTION 2. MEDICAL NECESSITY CRITERIA

Arthroscopic surgery of the knee is considered medically necessary when ALL of the
following criteria are met:

2.1 The member has a documented diagnosis of osteoarthritis of the knee (ICD-10
M17.0 through M17.9) or derangement of meniscus (ICD-10 M23.2 through M23.3)
established prior to the date of service.

2.2 The member has completed a minimum of six (6) months of supervised conservative
therapy, evidenced by at least one supervised physical therapy encounter documented
in each of six (6) or more distinct calendar months within the twenty-four (24)
months preceding the date of service, with no interruption between consecutive
documented encounters exceeding forty-five (45) days.

2.3 Diagnostic imaging of the affected knee (CPT 73721, 73722, or 73723) was
obtained subsequent to the completion of the conservative therapy trial described
in 2.2, and no earlier than the final documented conservative therapy encounter.

2.4 The member has not undergone arthroscopic surgery of the same knee within the
one hundred eighty (180) days preceding the date of service.

2.5 A body mass index (BMI) of less than 40.0 kg/m2 is documented within the twelve
(12) months preceding the date of service.

SECTION 3. DOCUMENTATION STANDARDS

All criteria in Section 2 must be substantiated by documentation present in the
member's medical record as of the date of service. Documentation entered into the
record after the date of service may be considered only upon reconsideration and
must be identified as such. Retrospective entry of conservative therapy encounters
without contemporaneous supporting documentation does not satisfy criterion 2.2.

SECTION 4. LIMITATIONS AND EXCLUSIONS

Arthroscopic lavage and debridement performed as a sole procedure for osteoarthritis
of the knee is considered not medically necessary, consistent with published evidence
demonstrating no benefit over placebo procedure.
